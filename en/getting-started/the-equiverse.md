# ☄️ The Equiverse?

Stables is building a simulation that mirrors the reality of horse racing. We aim to transform the interaction with horse racing **from gambling to gaming.**

Our goal is to create a global community of players engaged in an exciting horse racing fantasy game. To achieve this, Stables plans to develop a virtual simulation of a massive hippodrome, which will be the centerpiece of the platform and provide players with a realistic and immersive gaming experience.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/stales universe otherworld.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The platform will combine the **thrill of horse racing** with the **social aspects of gaming**. Along with immersive gameplay, you will have the opportunity to connect with other players and form guilds.

Overall, Stables aims to create a unique gaming experience that combines strategy, excitement, and social interaction. We aspire to become a leading player in the world of online gaming. So join us today and experience the thrill of racing like never before!
