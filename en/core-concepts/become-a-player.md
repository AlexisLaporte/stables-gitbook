# 🐴 Become a Player

At the beginning of the project, Stables will be accessed through a web terminal called MyStables, which will serve as the player control center. From there, you will be able to manage your horses, participate in races, and use your S-Points.

Later, we will release a more immersive mobile version of the game, allowing players to have the Equiverse at their fingertips.

## **Stables Pass**

At the time of the drop, by signing up on the website, you received a free Non-Transferable NFT (called Stables Pass) that you can hold forever as your membership pass for Stables. We may offer benefits related to this pass, with our earliest members being rewarded throughout the life of the project. An example : The OG Racers Event in January 2025.

## **Whitelist**

In the first months of Stables, the only way to guarantee access to an NFT is to be whitelisted, achieved through a series of quests that let you explore the lore of the Stables universe. Additionally, whitelisted players will receive extra perks, such as exclusive packs.

If you are not whitelisted, you may still have a chance to get an NFT during the private sales (registered members only) once the whitelisted sales are closed.

Later on, Stables will open to many more players and we will release new NFTs. Rest assured that our first collection offers the best value for money!
