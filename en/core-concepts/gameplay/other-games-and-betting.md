# Other games & Betting

Stables will regularly release new games and ways to explore the equiverse.

## Other games

* **Discord Game**: we're fond of community games on Discord and we're looking into releasing new things #teasing
* **Casual Mini-Game**: we're looking into creating other game than the Fantasy, even though this will remain the main game, to broaden our audience to more occasional players
* **3D Game**: for both the rendering of hybrid games in the Fantasy game, or as a new mini-game as presenter above, we're thinking about creating full 3D environments in existing metaverses and as our own asset in a 3D game

## Betting

What if hybrid races inside Stables could be opened to betting?&#x20;

We're opened to that, even though we would not mix game mechanics and betting, the later would be accessed through different interfaces.
