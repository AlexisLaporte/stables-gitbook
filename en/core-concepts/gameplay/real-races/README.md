# Real Races

The real horse associated with your NFT will race in real life, passively earning you currency (S-Points) and experience points for the NFTs. Only French races by PMU (called premium) are considered in the distribution.

The reward in S-Points is directly related to real-life rewards (called allocations). Since horses have long careers (up to 6 years), we took measures to control the amount of S-Points we distribute.

Stables has developed a mathematical model to determine the rewards for each race and each NFT, based on the following principles:

* When an NFT is created (sold), a set amount of S-Points is added to the global prize pool.
* Historical data was used to estimate the expected winnings of a typical horse over its career. This information allows us to distribute the prize pool evenly across all races for about 6 years (with most of it being released in 2-3 years).

For our first drop, each NFT will contribute 3.333 S-Points to the prize pool. This means the average horse would earn 3.333 S-Points over its career (if an average horse existed; in reality, some horses will earn more, and some less).
