# 🦄 Gameplay

Stables is building a simulation, an echo of the reality of horse racing.

It will be an immersive gaming experience that will let you take part in virtual horse races, where you can compete against other players from around the world.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/stables universe church.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For now, Stables will be accessed through a web terminal that serves as the player control center.

There will be, and there is already, many side games and quests, so that you can explore the lore of the universe and get to know more about the Equiverse.

The main games are Hybrid & Virtual Races, and your real horse NFT may also passively generate points.
