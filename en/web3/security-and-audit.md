# 🔐 Security & Audit

The Stables tech team places a strong emphasis on security and compliance, particularly for our Web3 assets.&#x20;

To screen funds on Tezos, we use Elliptic.&#x20;

We are in the process of managing all our addresses with Fireblocks, and private keys are not shared among the team.&#x20;

Our code undergoes regular review by external auditors such as Functori or RAID Square.
