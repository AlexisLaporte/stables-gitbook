# 🏦 Business Model

Stables revenues come from the sale of NFTs and future in-game purchases. We also plan to generate a small revenue from the secondary market (5% fee).

A part of it (10%) is forwarded to real horse owners through their respective associations Le Trot and France Galop (that's pure royalties).&#x20;

The rest is spent for the game development, roughly equally in product and marketing.

Stables is an innovative project powered and financed by PMU.&#x20;

We also spend a good share of the sale for the community of holders, this mean that we will keep on offering special gifts such as visits to hippodromes or create special events for the community.
